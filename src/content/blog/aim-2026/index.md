ROBOSECT is proud to sponsor the IEEE/ASME International Conference on Advanced Intelligent Mechatronics (AIM) 2026, taking place from July 7th to 10th, 2026, at Porto Antico – Magazzini del Cotone in Genoa, Italy.
During the conference, the ROBOSECT team will be attending the event and welcoming visitors at our exhibition stand. Whether you are interested in our robotic platforms, ongoing projects, or potential collaborations, feel free to stop by and ask us any questions.
We look forward to meeting researchers, engineers, students, and industry professionals from around the world and sharing the latest developments from ROBOSECT.
